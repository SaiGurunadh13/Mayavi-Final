Our Wings

We are excited to announce that in our club, we ensure to share and explore all the wings of gaming, virtual reality, augmented reality, and mixed reality with our members.

01ENGINE ARCHITECTURE & GAMEPLAY

Game Development

Game development is a captivating blend of artistry and technology, where worlds come alive and stories unfold. It encompasses the process of creating video games from concept to completion, engaging players in immersive experiences.

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Unity 3D
  • Gameplay Systems
  • C++ / C#
02SPATIAL COMPUTING & IMMERSIVE TECH

Extended Reality

Extended Reality (XR) is a revolutionary technology that merges the real and virtual worlds, creating immersive and interactive experiences. It encompasses Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR), blurring the boundaries between physical and digital realms.

  • Augmented Reality
  • Virtual Reality
  • Mixed Reality
  • WebXR SDK
033D DIGITAL CREATION & MOTION

Modelling and Animations

Modelling and animations are crucial aspects of digital content creation, bringing life and realism to virtual worlds. Modelling involves creating 3D models of characters, objects, and environments using specialized software. Skilled artists meticulously craft shape, texture, and details for maximum visual appeal.

  • Blender Pipeline
  • Character Rigging
  • Lighting & Shaders
  • 3D Simulation
04HUMAN-CENTERED & SPATIAL DESIGN

UI/UX Design

UI/UX, short for User Interface and User Experience, focuses on creating seamless and engaging digital interactions. UI encompasses the visual elements and layout of an interface, while UX focuses on the overall user journey and experience. A well-designed UI/UX strives to provide intuitive navigation.

  • Spatial Interfaces
  • Design Systems
  • Interaction Flows
  • Interactive Prototyping

The Mayavi Experience

At Mayavi, we believe that gaming is not just a hobby but an art form that can be mastered. Our club is dedicated to creating an immersive and inclusive environment where enthusiasts like you can come together, share ideas, and collaborate on exciting projects. By joining Mayavi, you open yourself up to a world of opportunities and benefits.

Let's take a look at some of the advantages of being a member:

01

Game Development

Unleash your creativity and bring your game ideas to life at Mayavi. Learn programming languages, game engines, and design principles to transform your dreams into playable realities.

02

AR Development

Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of Augmented Reality (AR) at Mayavi. Access workshops and training sessions to create interactive experiences that merge the virtual and physical worlds.

03

VR Development

Embark on a journey into Virtual Reality (VR) with Mayavi. Build captivating virtual environments, develop interactive gameplay mechanics, and transport users to extraordinary realms.

04

UI/UX Designing

Master the art of crafting visually stunning interfaces and optimizing user experiences at Mayavi. Elevate your games and designs with skills that make them truly standout and engage players.
